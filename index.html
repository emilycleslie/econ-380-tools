Econ 380 Tools

Interactive, student-facing tools for Intermediate Price Theory 1.

Math Proficiency
Five short exams covering the algebra and calculus this course runs on, and a review page for each. Take the exam on Learning Suite, review whatever it flags, retake it.
Magic Square
Moving between indirect utility, the expenditure function, and Marshallian and Hicksian demand. Six arrows, worked both directions.
Allocation mechanisms
Which part of the demand curve actually gets served under a price ceiling, and what that does to consumer surplus. Switch between highest-WTP, lowest-WTP, and lottery and watch the triangles move.

This repository holds student-facing materials only. Course planning files, readings, and administrative records live elsewhere.

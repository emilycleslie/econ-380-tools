How it works

Each one is a separate exam on Learning Suite. If you get seven out of eight or better, you're done with that exam. If you don't, come back here to review the material, then retake it. It draws fresh questions each time. Take it as many times as you need.

The five exams

Math Proficiency 1 · Exponents, logs, and simplifying expressions
Exponent and log rules, fractional and negative exponents, and collapsing a messy ratio into something short. The last one is the move you'll make in nearly every problem this semester.
Math Proficiency 2 · Derivatives and critical points
Power, product, and chain rules; derivatives of ln and e; finding a maximum or minimum and knowing which one you found. That last one is every optimization problem in the course.
Math Proficiency 3 · Partial derivatives
Differentiating with respect to one variable while the others sit still. This is where marginal utility comes from, and where the MRS starts.
Math Proficiency 4 · Total differentials and total derivatives
What happens to a function when everything moves at once — and how setting that to zero gives you the slope of an indifference curve.
Math Proficiency 5 · Solving the optimization system
Dividing two conditions to make the multiplier cancel, then substituting into the constraint. A process you'll go through many times.
